Description

Room details.

The top-floor double suite features a large, bright room overlooking the square on one side and the sea on the other. A spacious terrace, almost the same size as the room, completes the space. It is furnished in an essential and elegant style, with a marble fireplace and an open bathroom separated by two stone arches. It includes Wi-Fi, TV, air conditioning and heating.
