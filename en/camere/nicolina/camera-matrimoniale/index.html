Alleyway Room

Description

Room details.

Overlooking the narrow lane just above Vernazza's square, this double room with independent bathroom is a quiet and spacious solution. It is ideal for guests seeking a private stay one minute from the center. The meeting point is Vulnetia restaurant in Piazza Marconi: from the station, walk down the main street; once you reach the square, turn right and Vulnetia is the second restaurant.
